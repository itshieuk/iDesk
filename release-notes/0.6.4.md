## What's new?

* **TikTok, Reels and Shorts scroll like on the phone:** a two-finger scroll on a video feed is now one real finger flick, so each swipe moves exactly one video and the next one starts playing. Lists and pages still scroll smoothly.
* **Pinch to zoom:** pinch or spread two fingers on the trackpad to zoom web pages (50–300%) and photos or documents inside iOS apps, around the pointer.
* **Quit app completely:** an iOS app's window menu has *Quit app completely*. iOS can't unload an app from iDesk, so this saves everything and closes iDesk; open it again and your windows come back, without that app.
* **Links stay in iDesk:** YouTube, TikTok, Facebook… links on web pages no longer jump to the installed app on the iPhone, and `youtube://`-style links are ignored.
* **Laptop mode, redone:**
  * Turn the iPhone sideways and it becomes the display: the desktop fills the screen in the iPhone's own shape, clear of the Dynamic Island, with a small ✕ to leave.
  * Upright: a compact header with Stop, a copyable address with a status dot, the desktop preview, and a trackpad under it.
  * Two-finger scrolling works on the preview too.
* **iPad:** the toolbar on the iPad screen is centred instead of pushed to the right.
* Cleanup: shared code for touches, GitHub reports and feed scrolling.

## Confirmed working

* iPhone 17 Pro simulator (iOS 26.3): laptop mode upright and sideways, trackpad in laptop mode, pinch zoom on web pages.
* iPad Air 11-inch M2 simulator (iOS 18.3): the iPad screen and the external desktop in portrait and landscape.
* From earlier releases on a real iPhone: TikTok runs in a window, YouTube opens and browses.

## Known issues

* The new feed scrolling, pinch zoom in apps, *Quit app completely* and the link fix haven't been tried on a real iPhone yet: please report how they behave.
* YouTube (original app): videos may say "No stream". Many people can play with a modded build.
* Facebook may ask for a captcha at sign-in; signing in on the web first helps.
* Modded IPAs that patch code while running (some YouTube/Facebook tweaks) can make iOS close iDesk.
* Laptop mode goes over Wi-Fi: a little behind a cable, and only on the same network.

## Installation

1. Download **iDesk-0.6.4.ipa** below. It is unsigned.
2. Sign it with ESign, Feather or Scarlet and install it. From 0.6.1, *Settings › Help › Update* does this and keeps your iOS apps.
3. Connect a display: a USB-C → HDMI cable, Apple's Lightning → HDMI adapter, AirPlay to an Apple TV / AirPlay TV / Mac, or *Laptop* for a laptop's browser on the same Wi-Fi.

Needs iOS 17 or later (iPhone XS / XR and newer, or an iPad).

## Có gì mới (tiếng Việt)

* Lướt TikTok, Reels, Shorts bằng 2 ngón: mỗi lần vuốt chuyển đúng một video.
* Chụm / xoè 2 ngón để thu phóng trang web và ảnh trong app.
* *Tắt hẳn app* trong menu cửa sổ app.
* Link trên web không còn tự mở app YouTube, TikTok… trên iPhone.
* Chế độ laptop: xoay ngang iPhone để dùng làm màn hình; dọc thì có trackpad bên dưới.
* iPad: thanh công cụ nằm giữa.

## Issues

If anything goes wrong, tap **Send report** in iDesk after a crash, use **Report** on the Apps page for an app, or open an issue on GitHub.
